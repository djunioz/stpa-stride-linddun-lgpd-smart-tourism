This catalog presents the 26 causal scenarios developed in Step 4 of the \gls{stpa} analysis of Chapter 5 of the dissertation, registered in AppSTPA on 25 representative \glspl{hca}. For each scenario, Table~\ref{tab:cenarios} gives the context that triggers it, the main \gls{hca}, the set of \glspl{hca} explained by the scenario, including the main \gls{hca}, and the hazards registered for the scenario in the instrument of the analysis. The hazards column is not always the union of the hazards of the \glspl{hca} explained, as Section 5.5 of the dissertation notes. The codes TUR, SMA, BAC, OPE, and GER identify the Tourist, the Smartphone application, the Back-end, the Operations Team, and Company Management, and the numbers of the \glspl{hca} are the ones generated by AppSTPA.
