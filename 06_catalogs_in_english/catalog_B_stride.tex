This catalog registers the complete results of the STRIDE analysis of Step 6, presented in Section 6.2 of the dissertation. Table~\ref{tab:apxB_grupos} presents the 12 threat groups with their threat family, their STRIDE categories, the flows and the number of records consolidated in each group, and the hazards. Table~\ref{tab:apxB_controles} presents the 14 cybersecurity controls derived from the groups, with their statements and their relation to \gls{stpa}. In the statements, operator designates a member of the Operations Team, the staff of the controller, and not the data processor of the law. Table~\ref{tab:stride_registros} lists the 98 candidate threat records generated by the \gls{tmt}, with the \gls{tmt} identifier, the \gls{dfd} flow of the interaction, the STRIDE category, the title of the record in the tool, the decision of the review, the consolidated group of the applicable records, and the hazards. The records classified as not applicable carry the code of the justification. NA-2 indicates that the communication model of the design does not correspond to the one presupposed by the record, and NA-3 indicates that the record presupposes a delegation or reuse of security context that the design does not have. The four records generated on the interactions labeled Request were assigned to F1 or to F12 according to the entity that they concern.
